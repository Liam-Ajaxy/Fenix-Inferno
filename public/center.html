What is FENIX?

FENIX is an intelligence-focused web ecosystem featuring AI, open UI standards, and user-centered tools.

Can I customize my dashboard?

Yes! Visit your Settings > Layouts to personalize your dashboard experience.

Is there a mobile version?

FENIX is fully responsive and works on all modern mobile browsers.

You can contact support or post in the Community Forum under “Bug Reports”.
